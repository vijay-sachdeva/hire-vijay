Exhibit 7 — Operating beliefs

Five things I would bring to the first business review.

Meta · asset recovery

The bottleneck is never the technology. It's the table nobody owns.

900 sites, a third of the inventory, invisible to every system meant to see them. A governance problem in an engineering costume — worth $43M a year.

Meta · 2Africa

Align the incentive and the deal writes itself.

Three carriers didn't need persuading, they needed a trade: subsea cost for terrestrial expansion they already wanted. Structure beats advocacy.

Meta · AI evals

If you can't measure the pilot, you don't have a pilot.

Assistants were wrong most of the time and confident about it. Accuracy went 30% → 85% the moment measurement existed.

inCODE · spectrum

Price the market, not the asset.

The real question was never "what is this worth" but "what is it worth to us, here, against these bidders." Same distinction as a metrics report versus a business review.

Meta · backbone delivery

Speed comes from removing a handoff, not adding people.

The shortfall wasn't staffing; it was a seam between vendor updates and internal inventory that no tool crossed. One sync closed it.

DAYS 61–90

First use-case taxonomy from CRM, call reviews and pitch clinics — then a prioritization framework explicit about what AMER stops doing. A framework that only adds is a wish list.
